Your keyboard works too: A–F, 1–9, Enter, Backspace, U to undo.

How to play

  • Sort all 36 cards into four stacks running 9 down to 1. A finished stack becomes a solid colour bar and is locked.
  • Type two stacks to move a card: BE moves B's top card onto E.
  • Add a number to move a run: BE4 moves the top four cards, which must count down, like 9 8 7 6.
  • A card fits on a card one higher, or on an empty stack.
  • You can also drop one card anywhere, but that stack is then blocked . Nothing can go on it until that card moves away.
